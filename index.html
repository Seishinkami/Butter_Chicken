Garlic Butter Chicken with Cilantro Lime Rice

Juicy pan-seared chicken bathed in rich garlic butter, paired with fragrant, bright cilantro lime rice for the ultimate quick dinner.

Prep Time

15 mins

Cook Time

30 mins

Servings

4 portions

Difficulty

Easy

Ingredients

4
  • Chicken Breasts 4 pieces
  • Unsalted Butter 2 tbsp
  • Garlic (minced) 4 cloves
  • Olive Oil 1 tbsp
  • Basmati Rice 1.5 cups
  • Fresh Lime Juice 2 limes
  • Fresh Cilantro (chopped) 0.5 cup
  • Salt & Black Pepper To taste
Cooking Progress
0 of 3
Step 1

Prepare & Cook the Cilantro Lime Rice

Rinse basmati rice thoroughly. Cook with 3 cups of salted water until fluffy. Fluff with a fork, then fold in fresh lime juice, lime zest, and finely chopped cilantro. Cover and keep warm.

Step 2

Sear the Seasoned Chicken

Pat chicken breasts dry with paper towels and season generously with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken 6-8 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through (165°F internal temperature).

Step 3

Make Garlic Butter & Combine

Reduce heat to low. Add butter and minced garlic to the skillet, spooning the melted garlic butter over the chicken continuously for 1-2 minutes until fragrant. Serve sliced over cilantro lime rice.
