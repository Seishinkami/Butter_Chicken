Garlic Butter Chicken
with Cilantro Lime Rice

Juicy pan-seared chicken breasts basted in rich garlic butter, paired with fresh, citrusy cilantro lime basmati rice.

Prep Time

15 mins

Cook Time

30 mins

Total Time

45 mins

Adjust Servings

4 Servings

Ingredients

Click to cross off

For the Chicken

  • Chicken breasts 4
  • Unsalted butter 2 tbsp
  • Garlic, minced 4 cloves
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  • Kosher salt 1 tsp
  • Black pepper ½ tsp
  • Paprika ½ tsp

For the Rice

  • Basmati rice 1½ cups
  • Broth or water 2¼ cups
  • Fresh limes 2
  • Fresh cilantro ½ cup
  • Fresh parsley ¼ cup
  • Kosher salt ¾ tsp

Preparation Steps

Click step to mark complete
1

Cook & Steam Rice

Rinse basmati rice until water runs clear. In a medium saucepan, combine rice, broth, and salt. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let sit covered for 10 minutes before fluffing with a fork.

2

Sear Chicken Breasts

Pat chicken breasts dry with paper towels. Season generously on both sides with kosher salt, black pepper, and paprika. Heat olive oil in a large heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken for 5–6 minutes per side until golden brown.

3

Garlic Butter Basting

Turn heat down to medium-low. Add butter and minced garlic to the skillet. Once butter foams, tilt the pan and continuously spoon the warm garlic butter over the chicken for 2–3 minutes until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).

4

Finish Rice & Assemble

Fold lime zest, lime juice, fresh cilantro, and chopped parsley into the cooked basmati rice. Slice the chicken into strips and serve over a generous bed of warm cilantro lime rice, drizzling remaining garlic butter from the pan over top.
