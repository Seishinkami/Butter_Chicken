Garlic Butter Chicken with Cilantro Lime Rice

Juicy pan-seared chicken, fragrant rice, and a bright squeeze of lime.

Jump to recipe

Dinner, simplified

Big flavor, one very good plate.

Rich, golden garlic butter chicken meets fluffy cilantro lime rice in a meal that feels special without asking much of your evening.

The fresh herbs and citrus cut through every buttery bite, making this a vibrant, satisfying dinner for any night of the week.

Gather your ingredients

The good stuff

Quantities update as you adjust servings.

For the chicken

  • Boneless, skinless chicken breasts4 breasts
  • Unsalted butter2 tbsp
  • Garlic, finely minced4 cloves
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Kosher salt1 tsp
  • Freshly cracked black pepper½ tsp
  • Paprika (optional)½ tsp

For the cilantro lime rice

  • Basmati rice, rinsed1½ cups
  • Water or chicken broth2¼ cups
  • Fresh limes2 limes
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped½ cup
  • Fresh parsley, chopped¼ cup
  • Kosher salt¾ tsp

Cook with confidence

Step-by-step

Your progress0 of 5
1

Cook and rest the rice

Bring the rinsed basmati, water or broth, and salt to a boil. Cover, reduce to low, and cook until tender. Remove from the heat and let it steam, covered, for 10 minutes.

Pro tip: Resting lets the grains finish steaming without turning sticky.

2

Season and sear the chicken

Pat the chicken dry, then season generously with salt, pepper, and optional paprika. Heat olive oil in a cast-iron skillet and sear until golden on both sides.

Pro tip: Leave the chicken undisturbed for a deep, restaurant-style crust.

3

Add garlic butter and baste

Lower the heat and add butter with the minced garlic. Spoon the sizzling butter over the chicken until it is glossy and cooked through, then rest for five minutes.

Pro tip: Add garlic only after lowering the heat so it stays fragrant, not bitter.

4

Fluff with cilantro and lime

Fluff the rested rice gently with a fork. Fold in lime zest, fresh lime juice, chopped cilantro, and half of the parsley until bright and aromatic.

Pro tip: Zest your limes before juicing for the cleanest, easiest prep.

5

Plate with fresh finishing touches

Spoon cilantro lime rice onto warm plates and nestle the sliced chicken alongside. Drizzle with the skillet butter, then finish with parsley and lime wedges.

Pro tip: A final squeeze of lime wakes up every savory, buttery note.
