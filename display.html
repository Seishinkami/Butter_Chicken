Weeknight Special

Garlic Butter Chicken
with Cilantro Lime Rice

Juicy pan-seared chicken, fragrant rice, and a bright squeeze of lime.

Prep Time

15 mins

Cook Time

30 mins

Servings

4

Ingredients

Yield: 4 Servings

For the Chicken

  • Chicken breasts 4
  • Unsalted butter 2 tbsp
  • Garlic, minced 4 cloves
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  • Kosher salt 1 tsp
  • Black pepper ½ tsp
  • Paprika ½ tsp

For the Rice

  • Basmati rice 1½ cups
  • Broth / Water 2¼ cups
  • Fresh limes 2
  • Fresh cilantro ½ cup
  • Fresh parsley ¼ cup
  • Kosher salt ¾ tsp

Preparation

1

Cook & Steam Rice

Bring basmati, broth, and salt to a boil. Cover, turn to low, cook until tender, then rest covered for 10 minutes.

2

Sear Chicken

Season chicken with salt, pepper, and paprika. Sear in hot olive oil in a heavy skillet until golden brown on both sides.

3

Garlic Butter Baste

Reduce heat, melt butter with minced garlic, and continuously spoon the butter over chicken until fully cooked.

4

Finish & Serve

Fluff rice with a fork and fold in lime juice, zest, cilantro, and parsley. Plate with sliced chicken and drizzle butter on top.
